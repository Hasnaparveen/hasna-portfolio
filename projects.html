WATER MANAGEMENT · PROTOTYPE

DropWise

A water-management prototype concept designed to help households and shared spaces become more aware of water usage and possible wastage.

Problem it explores

Water can be wasted when tanks overflow, leaks go unnoticed, or usage is difficult to track. DropWise explores how timely alerts and simple usage information could help people respond earlier.

Concept features

  • Tank-level awareness
  • Potential overflow, low-water, and leak alerts
  • Water usage and cost insights

This is a prototype/demo. Feature availability may vary; this description presents the project concept rather than claiming a fully deployed monitoring system.

DIGITAL SAFETY · DEMONSTRATION

Scam Detection

A demonstration concept exploring how digital scam indicators might be presented to help users pause and assess suspicious messages or interactions.

Problem it explores

Digital scams can use urgent language, misleading requests, and suspicious links to pressure people into acting quickly. This project explores the idea of making potential warning signs easier to notice.

Project focus

  • Exploring potential scam indicators
  • Presenting information in a clear, accessible way
  • Encouraging users to verify suspicious requests

This is a demonstration concept, not a guarantee of scam detection. Users should independently verify suspicious messages and payment requests.
